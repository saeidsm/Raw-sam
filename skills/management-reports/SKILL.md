Ordinary concrete and all retained substrates remain active. Calculate active progress from consistent numerator/denominator; never credit old yellow-panel installation as new two-tone work. - Latest action plan supplies crew requirements. Transfer overdue, unclosed tasks once by stable ID; no duplicate crews across overlapping fronts. Separate execution and handling. Library has no current action plan: use dated user-confirmed front requirements. Weekly actual crew must be current, not a stale summary block.
- Finance is mandatory intake: contract amount, latest statement/adjustment number, submitted/reviewed/employer-approved amounts and dates, advances, actual payments and reconciliation. These are separate stages. Iron differential is an advance within adjustment: deduct the amount actually paid at adjustment settlement, never add it as an independent entitlement. Approval does not establish payment; null payments are not zero and net payable cannot be derived without deductions/payment evidence.
- Tick plus ongoing work, repair or residual quantity does not close an item. Receipt does not equal installation. Preserve observation dates.

## Report and QA
- Preserve the latest portrait A4 PPTX reference: two logos, Vazirmatn, Persian RTL, Persian digits and at least1cm safe margins. Use the bundled assets/fonts/Vazirmatn-Regular.ttf and Vazirmatn-Bold.ttf when the host lacks this font; preserve the accompanying OFL.txt. Native editable text, tables and numbers; images for real maps/photos only. Employer copy is concise, firm and technical. Real unresolved ambiguity uses an asterisk with a private explanation, never invented certainty or alarming editorial placeholders.
- Library first cards: overall, installation, installed library facade glass, actual weekly crew. Split curtain/clamp charts into equal columns. Always show installed/total. Keep skylight and agriculture quantities out of the library facade glass sum.
- Compare four consecutive published periods. Combine installed-to-date, current growth and remaining with previous/current rates. Quantity forecast = remaining in the SAME scope / current-week installation. No numeric completion time for zero/negative rate. Distinguish rate-based weeks from contractual dates and final handover; do not combine field remaining with another ledger's installation rate.
- Map priorities to actual obstructions and ready fronts. Keep factory clamp repair, regulation and waterproofing until closed. Do not reinstate the deleted section about reading curve numbers. Historical resource curves must not be represented as current active-scope demand.
- Render and inspect every page, including the first blue box and footer. Verify exact sums, chart lengths, actual template and font embedding. Test text/table edits and change native chart source cells in a disposable copy, save and reopen. Numeric charts must use native PowerPoint charts with embedded workbooks, formulas for derived quantities, and cell-linked Persian labels/titles. Do not deliver static bars or detached numeric labels. Read [native chart implementation and QA](references/native-charts.fa.md) when authoring or updating charts.
- Store source ledger, decisions, calculations, QA and named dated output. Read the user guide for missing-input handling. Past numbers are dated evidence, not constants for future weeks.
